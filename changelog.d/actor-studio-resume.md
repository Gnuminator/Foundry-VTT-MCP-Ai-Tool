### Module

- **Resume an unfinished character (Actor Studio fork 2.10.5-aitool.7):** a player who closes Actor Studio or reloads the page in the middle of the advancements no longer loses the character. A box on the next page load, or an orange bar on the sheet, reopens Actor Studio and asks only the choices that were not finished. The player guide's "You closed the builder halfway" row says so. This reaches the Pi with the stage 13 pin for `2.10.5-aitool.7`; until then the guide text is ahead of the Pi.

### Module

- **Automatic party:** Foundry now keeps a dnd5e primary party by itself. The active GM's client makes a Group actor "Party" when there is none and adds every character a player owns, both at world load and as new characters are made or get a player owner. NPCs and actors no player owns stay out, nobody is added twice, and each batch of additions shows in Recent Changes with Undo (Undo takes back the newest batch; to take out an older character, remove it on the party sheet and it stays out). A deleted party is made again. The setting "AI Tool: Add player characters to the party" (default on) turns it off. "Ask the party" no longer needs a party made by hand. The GM guide's party step is replaced by one line.

### Docs

- **Away game preflight:** `scripts/away/away-preflight.ps1` is a read-only check for the day before an in-person night away from home. It tests the game's address from the laptop's network, Discord, the TV screen, the browser and power, and prints a pack list. New GM guide "A night at someone else's place" in English and Danish, linked from the runbook (away-game-preflight).

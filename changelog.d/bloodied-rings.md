### Admin

- **Ring look in `plan-token-rings`:** `look: "steel"` sets the table's ring look (steel ring,
  dark background, subject scale 0.8) in the same guarded plan as turning the ring on, for actors
  and for tokens already placed, and plans rings that are on without that look. Undo restores the
  old colours and scale. Tried on a copy of the Strahd world (I-093).

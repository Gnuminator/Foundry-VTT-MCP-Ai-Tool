### Module

- **Combat mood:** a new switch, "AI Tool: Combat mood (writes)", off by default. With it on, a fight
  that starts on the scene you are viewing applies that scene's mood preset named "Combat"
  (darkness, light, weather, music), and the earlier look and music come back when the fight ends.
  A scene without a "Combat" preset is left alone. Each change shows in Changes with an Undo. The
  combat start and end events now carry the scene id.

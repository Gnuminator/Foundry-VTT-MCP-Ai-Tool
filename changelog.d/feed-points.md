### Dashboard

- **Recent Changes, token moves (changes-token-moves):** on the Everyone tab a run of moves of the same token by the same person is one row ("Ogre moved 6 times", newest time, Undo takes back the newest move). A drag across the map no longer pushes the real changes out of the list. Any other change in between ends the run.
- **Feed, say what changed (feed-gm-change-wording):** the chip on a change made in Foundry names the feature behind it ("actor change", "page reveal") instead of "gm-change", in the new dashboard and on the old page (`/legacy`). The line beside it still says what changed and on whom.

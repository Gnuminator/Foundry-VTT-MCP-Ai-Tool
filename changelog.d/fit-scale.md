### Play

- **Fit tokens to the map's scale:** `plan-token-change` has a new action `fit-scale`. On a town or
  overland map with 16 to 200 ft per square, it sizes each token so a creature covers its real
  footprint (a medium creature is 5 ft, a large one 10 ft, from the actor's size), with a floor of
  0.25 squares so tokens stay visible. Name tokens, or leave them out for the whole scene. A 5 ft
  scene is left alone. Plan, confirm and Undo like any token change.

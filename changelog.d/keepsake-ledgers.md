### Players

- **Keepsake ledgers:** a player can pick a keepsake for their character on `/me` ("My keepsakes"): a book of the names of those who went down, a purse of coin won and lost, or a count of one kind of event. Each fills itself from the public play log, shows only what the whole table has seen, and is written to the player's vault as `My keepsakes.md`. It needs the player vaults to be on. A count of long rests waits for the module to log rests (item `keepsake-ledgers`).

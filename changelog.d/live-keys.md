### Dashboard

- **Live keys:** on the live strip (the dashboard, `/gm` and the live screen) N is next turn, U undo, D jumps to the damage amount, R asks the party to roll and H shows the next handout. `?` opens a key sheet. A key presses the strip's own button, so every write still asks first, and no key fires while typing, with Ctrl, Alt or Meta held, or while a dialog is open (item live-keys).

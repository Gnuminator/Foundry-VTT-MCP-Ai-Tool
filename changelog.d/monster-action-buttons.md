### Dashboard

- **Monster action buttons on the live strip:** pick one creature that is not a player character and up to four of its weapon attacks show as buttons under the pick bar. A click rolls the attack against the creature you targeted in Foundry (the roll and its damage appear in Foundry's chat as normal), shows one result line, and puts the damage on the target only after a confirm, unless "Apply damage without asking" is ticked. With no target, or more than one, it says so and rolls nothing (I-165).

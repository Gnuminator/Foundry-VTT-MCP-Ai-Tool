### Build tools

- **Practice room for new players:** `plan-room-setup` with `action: practice` makes a generic training
  scene, a training dummy, one practice hero per player (owned by that player, with a sword attack and
  a cantrip), a step list journal (English and Danish pages) and a begun combat in one plan; Undo removes all of it until someone has played in the room (then the GM deletes it by hand). New
  player guide page "The practice room" in English and Danish (idea 2.14).

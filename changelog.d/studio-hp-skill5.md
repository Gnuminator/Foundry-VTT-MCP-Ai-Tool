### Tests (studio-fighter17-hp)

- **Actor Studio hit points explained:** the test kit's one open Studio finding, a level 17 Fighter ending at 145 of 157 hit points, is now a known entry: the system adds each level's hit points at the Constitution modifier the hero has at that moment and does not raise the current value when an ability score increase raises the modifier later (Actor Studio does not touch hit points).
- **Module pages say `2.10.5-aitool.5`:** the foundry-modules skill table and the Actor Studio page match what stage 13 put on the Pi, and mention the Select Spells window.

### Tarokka (tarokka-gm-choice)

- **A reading the GM chooses:** `plan-tarokka-import` takes the five cards the GM picked (`tomeCard`,
  `holySymbolCard`, `sunswordCard`, `allyCard`, `strahdLocationCard`) and stores them as the current
  reading with no draw and without the tarokka-reading module. The Tarokka drawer has a **Choose the
  cards…** form for it. Cards are checked against their deck and cannot repeat; the previous reading is
  archived and the change can be undone. Reveals already go one position at a time.

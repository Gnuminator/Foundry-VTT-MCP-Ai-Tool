### Discord bot

- **Voice memos into prep:** send the bot a voice message in a direct message and `node scripts/voice-memos.mjs` turns it, with the local speech to text and no paid service, into a dated note with the NPCs and places you named linked in `Prep/Voice memos` in the GM vault. Only the owner (or `DISCORD_MEMO_USER_IDS`) can send memos.

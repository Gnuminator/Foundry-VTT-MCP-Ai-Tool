# Foundry basics

These pages explain Foundry itself, in plain words, with pictures from Foundry 14 and the D&D 5e
system. They are written for a GM who has never used Foundry. Read them in this order with Foundry
open in a test world, and try each thing as you read. Players only need the first three.

| #   | Page                                                  | What you learn                                               |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | [A tour of the Foundry screen](screen-tour.md)        | What each part of the screen is, and where the buttons live  |
| 2   | [Foundry glossary](glossary.md)                       | The words Foundry uses, one or two lines each                |
| 3   | [Worlds and joining](worlds-and-joining.md)           | A world is a campaign; how you start it and how players join |
| 4   | [Users and permissions](users-and-permissions.md)     | The four roles, and who may see or change what               |
| 5   | [Scenes](scenes.md)                                   | Maps, viewing a scene and activating it for everyone         |
| 6   | [Actors](actors.md)                                   | Characters and monsters, their sheets and who owns them      |
| 7   | [Tokens](tokens.md)                                   | An actor on the map, its settings and what it can see        |
| 8   | [Journal entries](journals.md)                        | Notes and handouts, and showing a page to the players        |
| 9   | [Chat and dice](chat-and-dice.md)                     | The chat log, rolling dice and private GM rolls              |
| 10  | [Combat](combat.md)                                   | The combat tracker, initiative, turns and rounds             |
| 11  | [Layers, walls and lighting](walls-and-lighting.md)   | The scene controls by layer, what blocks sight, and light    |
| 12  | [Measuring and templates](measuring-and-templates.md) | The ruler and spell areas                                    |
| 13  | [Compendiums](compendiums.md)                         | The libraries of monsters, items and spells                  |
| 14  | [Modules](modules.md)                                 | Add-ons, and turning them on and off in a world              |
| 15  | [Playlists and sound](playlists.md)                   | Music and sound effects                                      |

Pages 1 to 10 are the ones for your first hour. Pages 11 to 15 you can read later, when you need them.

These pages describe Foundry 14 with the D&D 5e system 6.x. If a button is not where a page says, the
page may be out of date: tell whoever keeps this tool and they will redo the pictures.

For what Foundry AI Tool adds on top of Foundry, start with the [GM guides](../gm/README.md).

# Foundry glossary

Short answers, in alphabetical order. Where a page covers the topic, the term links to it.

**Activity.** In the D&D 5e system, one thing an item or feature can do, such as an attack, a saving throw or a heal. Weapons, spells and many other items have an **Activities** tab, and using the item runs its activity. See [Actors](actors.md).

**Active Effect.** A change that sits on an actor or item and alters its numbers while it is turned on, such as a condition or a spell bonus. Actors, weapons, spells and many other items have an **Effects** tab.

**Advancement.** An **Advancement** tab on class, species and background items in the D&D 5e system. It lists what a character gets from that item as they level up.

**Ambient Light.** A light you place on a scene yourself. Draw one with **Draw Light Source** in the **Lighting Controls**. See [Walls and lighting](walls-and-lighting.md).

**Ambient Sound.** A sound you place on a scene yourself, using the **Ambient Sound Controls**. Not the same as a [playlist](playlists.md).

**Assistant Gamemaster.** A user role just below Gamemaster. It sees everything as an owner but cannot open some settings. See [Users and permissions](users-and-permissions.md).

**Canvas.** The map area in the middle of the screen. See [A tour of the Foundry screen](screen-tour.md).

**Combat Encounter.** One fight in the **Combat Encounters** tab, with its turn order and rounds. See [Combat](combat.md).

**Compendium.** A locked shelf of ready-made documents that comes with the system or a module, such as monsters and spells. See [Compendiums](compendiums.md).

**Core.** Foundry itself, without any game system or module added. The **Core** category in Game Settings holds its settings.

**Directory.** The list inside a sidebar tab, for example the Actors directory. Entries can be sorted into folders.

**Document.** Anything Foundry stores as its own thing: an actor, item, scene, journal entry, playlist, macro and so on.

**Fog of War.** The part of a scene a player has not seen yet. The **Lighting Controls** have a **Reset Fog of War** button that wipes what players have explored. See [Walls and lighting](walls-and-lighting.md).

**Folder.** A way to group entries in a directory. Right-click a folder for more actions.

**Game Master.** The top user role. It has full control of the world. Also called Gamemaster. See [Users and permissions](users-and-permissions.md).

**Group (actor).** An actor type in D&D 5e that stands for the party and lists its members. See [Actors](actors.md).

**Hotbar.** The row of macro slots at the bottom centre of the screen. See [A tour of the Foundry screen](screen-tour.md).

**Item.** Something an actor can have: a weapon, a spell, a feature, a potion. Loose items sit in the **Items** tab. See [Actors](actors.md).

**Journal Entry.** A note or handout with one or more pages. See [Journals](journals.md).

**Level.** A newer Foundry 14 idea: one scene can have several levels, such as a ground floor and a cellar. Do not confuse it with character level.

**Macro.** A saved shortcut that runs a chat message or a script. It has a type, **Chat** or **Script**. Put it on the hotbar to use it with one key.

**Module.** An add-on that changes or extends Foundry. You switch modules on and off per world. See [Modules](modules.md).

**Note (map pin).** A pin on the map that opens a journal entry. Made with **Create Map Note** in the **Journal Notes** controls. See [Journals](journals.md).

**Ownership.** Who may see or edit one document: None, Limited, Observer or Owner. See [Users and permissions](users-and-permissions.md).

**Pause.** The GM presses Space to freeze the game. A **GAME PAUSED** banner shows and players cannot move tokens.

**Permission.** A thing a user role is allowed to do in the world, such as **Create Actors** or **Use Drawing Tools**. See [Users and permissions](users-and-permissions.md).

**Ping.** A pulse on the map that you make by clicking and holding on it while a Token Controls tool is active. Others see where you point.

**Placeable.** Any object that sits on a scene: a token, tile, drawing, wall, light, sound, region or note. The **Placeables** tab lists them.

**Playlist.** A group of sound tracks in the **Playlists** tab. See [Playlists and sound](playlists.md).

**Region.** An area on a scene. In Foundry 14, spell areas are drawn as regions. See [Measuring and templates](measuring-and-templates.md).

**Roll Table / Rollable Table.** A list of results that Foundry picks from at random when you draw. The tab is called **Rollable Tables**.

**Scene.** One map with its walls, lights and tokens. See [Scenes](scenes.md).

**Setup.** The screen you see when no world is running. You launch worlds from here and install systems and modules. See [Worlds and joining](worlds-and-joining.md).

**Sidebar.** The column of tabs on the right edge of the game screen. See [A tour of the Foundry screen](screen-tour.md).

**System.** The rules package for a world. This campaign uses the D&D 5e system (dnd5e), which provides the character sheets and the rules.

**Template.** The shape that shows the area of a spell or effect. In Foundry 14 it is a region. See [Measuring and templates](measuring-and-templates.md).

**Token.** The piece on the map that stands for an actor. See [Tokens](tokens.md).

**Trusted Player.** A user role just above Player. It can draw on the map, create journal entries and browse files by default. See [Users and permissions](users-and-permissions.md).

**Wall.** A line on a scene that blocks movement, sight or light, depending on its type. Doors and windows are walls too. See [Walls and lighting](walls-and-lighting.md).

**World.** One campaign: its scenes, actors, journals and users. You launch one world at a time. See [Worlds and joining](worlds-and-joining.md).

# First 15 minutes

This is the one page to read first. Everything else is there when you need it, and none of it has to
be learned in advance. You can stop after any step and come back.

## What this is

Foundry is the game on your screen: the map, the characters, the dice. Claude is an AI you can
chat with, and this tool lets it look at your Foundry game and help you run it. You stay in charge. For most game changes Claude shows you a plan first and you say yes, and they land in Recent Changes with an Undo. A few things, like rolls, chat and new NPCs, happen straight away; [Asking Claude](asking-claude.md) lists them.

## Four steps

1. **Get Claude talking to the game (5 minutes).** Follow
   [Connect Claude Desktop to the server](connect-claude-desktop.md) once. If the tool admin has
   already set up your PC, skip this and ask them to confirm.
2. **Look around Foundry (5 minutes).** Open Foundry, launch the world and join as the Game
   Master. Click through the tabs on the right and the buttons on the left. You cannot break
   anything by looking. The longer tour is [Your first hour as GM](first-hour.md), for later.
3. **Ask Claude one question (3 minutes).** In Claude Desktop, ask something that only reads, for
   example: "Which characters are in the world, and what level are they?" Reading never changes
   the game. More ideas are in [Asking Claude](asking-claude.md).
4. **See what a change looks like (2 minutes).** Open the dashboard on your second screen (the
   tool admin gives you its address) and find **Recent Changes**. That is where every planned change
   Claude makes is listed, each with an **Undo**. Nothing to do there yet, just know where it is.

**If you get stuck on game night, [the runbook](game-night-runbook.md) lists what to do when
something fails. If you are stuck at any other time, ask your [GM coach](gm-coach.md) in Claude.**

## What you can ignore for now

You do not need the dashboard panels, the feature switches, the Tarokka drawer, the Obsidian vault
or the cookbook yet. They are in the sidebar when a session needs them.

## Where to go next

- **Before session 0:** [Prepare session 0](prepare-session-0.md), about 30 minutes, once.
- **Before each game night:** [Before each session](before-session.md), about 15 minutes.
- **Something broke:** [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
- **Everything else:** the [GM guides](README.md).

Claude can also answer questions about Foundry itself, such as "how do I start combat?". Set up
the [GM coach](gm-coach.md) once and ask it whenever a button is unclear.

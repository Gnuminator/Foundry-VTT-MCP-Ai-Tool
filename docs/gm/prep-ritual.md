# Let's prep: 30 minutes, five steps

The **Foundry prep ritual** is a skill for your own Claude account. You say "let's prep" and Claude
walks you through the same five steps every time, one question at a time, so nothing gets skipped.
Claude only reads your game and suggests. You decide the story, and nothing changes in Foundry
without your yes.

## Add it

The skill is the file `foundry-prep-ritual.zip`, attached to each release download. Add it like the [GM coach](setup-gm-coach.md): Claude
Desktop, Settings, Plugins, and add the file. Then start a new chat and switch on the Foundry connectors
**foundry-mcp** and **foundry-mcp-prep** in the **+** menu, then **Plugins**.

## The five steps

1. **Last session in one screen.** A short "Previously on" from your recorded session. You say if
   something is missing.
2. **Loose ends.** Up to five open threads: quests, promises, questions the party left hanging.
   You pick the two or three you want to touch.
3. **This night's scenes.** You pick two to four scenes from your world and say what you want to
   happen in each.
4. **Encounter budget.** For each fight, Claude gets the XP budget for your party, compares the
   creatures on the scene with it and says whether the fight looks under, near or over. It is an
   estimate, and Claude suggests one change if it is off.
5. **Handouts to queue.** You choose which handouts to have ready. Nothing is shown to players
   during prep.

## The end: ready checklist

Claude runs the pre-flight check (`get-preflight`) and gives you a short checklist: scenes,
threads, fights, handouts and what is still red, most important first. If the pre-flight shows
errors, Claude tells you what to fix before calling the prep done.

## Tips

- Say "skip step 4" if there is no fight. It goes on the final list so you remember it.
- You can answer in Danish. Claude keeps the English game terms.
- For a plain read of the same facts without Claude, open the **Prep** drawer in the dashboard
  ([Get ready for next session](cookbook.md#get-ready-for-next-session)).

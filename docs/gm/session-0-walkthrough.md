# Session 0 walkthrough script

A one page script for the recorded part of session 0, where you show the players the tool on a
screen share. It takes about ten minutes. Say what you click, click it, and move on: nobody needs
to understand how it works yet. Nothing here shows book text or campaign secrets, so you can
record all of it.

Before you start: Foundry open in a tab as GM, the dashboard open in another tab (see [The
dashboard](dashboard.md)), and the players' links sent ([Send the players their links](send-players-their-links.md)).
The dashboard shows "GM Actions off" in a few places. That is correct for session 0: the tool
changes nothing in the world until you switch it on.

## 1. The dashboard, your side (2 minutes)

1. Share the dashboard tab. Point at the three phase buttons at the top: **Before**, **During**,
   **After**. They follow the evening and you rarely need to click them.
2. Open the **Theme** list and pick **The Veil**. The look changes for you and for every player page.
   Say that the players see the same look on their phones.
3. Point at **Pre-flight** (the check before a session) and **Recent Changes** (everything the tool
   changed, each with an **Undo** button). Say: nothing changes in the world without a plan you confirm,
   and anything can be undone.

## 2. The player page, what the players see (3 minutes)

1. Open the dashboard address with `/player` at the end. This is the page the players keep open.
2. Click your own name under **Who are you?** (or **Skip**).
3. Show the **You** card: your character's hit points, and **Open sheet**.
4. Show **Upload my portrait**: pick a picture, choose **Portrait and token**, and it replaces the
   character's picture in Foundry. It is optional.
5. Scroll to **Party stash**: the group's gold and what each character carries. Type a word in the
   search box to find an item.
6. Say that during a fight the **Combat order** and **Live Feed** move to the top by themselves.
   Enemies show no hit points here, so there is nothing to spoil.

## 3. My character on a phone (1 minute)

1. Open the `/me` link. It is a read-only sheet that stays up to date: hit points, spell slots,
   skills, and an **On my turn** card with the actions everyone has.
2. Click **Paper 2024** to show the print layout. Click **At the table** to go back.

## 4. Asking Claude (2 minutes)

1. Show the D&D helper ([The D&D helper](../player/dnd-helper.md)). Type a question a new player would
   ask, for example "I am new to D&D. What kind of character fits how I like to play?"
2. Say that Claude reads the table's books and the character sheet, and that it can suggest but it
   does not change anything unless you or the player confirm.

## 5. Making a character with Actor Studio (2 minutes, then they do it at home)

1. In Foundry, open the **Actors** tab (the person icon) and click the **Actor Studio** button.
2. On the **Abilities** tab, open the ability method list and pick one. With two methods on
   there is no default: the tab stays empty until a method is picked. The standard array (15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8)
   is the quick one.
3. Show the tabs **Species**, **Background** and **Class**: pick one in each, and say the description of
   every choice shows under the list.
4. Say that the builder makes a level 1 character. After **Create Character** the sheet shows a
   **Level Up** button: two clicks takes it to level 3, where the subclass is chosen.
5. Point the players at [Make your character](../player/make-a-character.md).

## Good to know

- A new wizard gets no Spellbook item from the Player's Handbook module. Add one by hand if
  a player asks.
- Players cannot delete a character they made. Delete test characters yourself, as GM.
- If a page looks stale on a phone, reload it. Live updates return within a few seconds.

# Player cookbook

Short how-tos for the extra bits around Foundry. Everything in Foundry itself works as usual. What
the tool records and which parts reach an AI is in the [player guide](README.md).

The player page is live behind the email-code login. The GM sends you its address.

## Pick your name on the player page

- **Do:** the first time you open the player page, click your name at the top. It then says
  "Playing as ...". Clicked the wrong one? Click **not you?** and pick again.
- **Why:** handouts meant only for you show only after you pick your name. The pick also tells the
  GM which handouts you have opened.
- **Tip:** your pick is remembered in that browser. On a new computer or browser, pick again.

## Follow the fight

- **Look at:** **Combat order** on the player page: who acts when, whose turn it is, and the round.
- **Good to know:** your party has HP bars. Monsters show no numbers, only a rough health word
  (healthy, bloodied, critical or down) unless the person who runs the tool switched that off, and a creature whose name you
  have not learned is "Unknown creature". Hidden creatures are not listed at all.

## Read a handout

- **Do:** open **Handouts** on the player page and click a title. You can also find the same page
  in Foundry's Journal tab.
- **"just for you":** the GM revealed it to you (or a few of you) only. Other players do not see it
  on their page, and in Foundry only you can open it. Share it at the table if your character
  would.
- **Tip:** nothing new? Handouts appear within a few seconds of the GM revealing them. Reload the
  page if one seems missing.

## Know when the session is recorded

- **Good to know:** when the person who runs the tool types `/record start` in Discord, the voice
  chat is recorded until `/record stop`, and the bot posts a notice when it starts. If you said no,
  your track is deleted after each session and the notes are written without you.
- **Tip:** want something off the record? Ask the GM; see
  [having your data removed](README.md#having-your-data-removed).

## Ask the GM, not the AI

Only the GM talks to Claude today, in their own Claude app. If you want to know something the tool
keeps track of, ask the GM. Good questions:

- "How much damage did I deal last session?" (the play stats per character)
- "Which handouts do we have so far?"
- "Can you send us a recap of last session?" (a spoiler-free recap made for players)
- "What did the letter from last week say again?"

## Roll when the GM asks

When the GM asks you for a roll, a button shows up on the player page, and on My character if you opened your private link. Press it and your roll goes to the table. It uses Foundry's own roll, so conditions and advantage on your character apply.

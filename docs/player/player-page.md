# Using the player page

Short how-tos for the extra bits around Foundry. Everything in Foundry itself works as usual: the
player page and My character are optional extras next to it, never a replacement. What the tool
records and which parts reach an AI is in the [player guide](README.md). Want ready-made requests
for the D&D helper (planning a character, levelling up, rules)? See the
[D&D helper cookbook](cookbook.md).

The player page is live behind the email-code login. The GM sends you its address.

## Pick your name on the player page

- **Do:** the first time you open the player page, click your name at the top. It then says
  "Playing as ...". Clicked the wrong one? Click **Not you? Change** and pick again.
- **Why:** handouts meant only for you show only after you pick your name. The pick also tells the
  GM which handouts you have opened.
- **Tip:** your pick is remembered in that browser. On a new computer or browser, pick again.

## Follow the fight

- **Look at:** **Combat order** on the player page: who acts when, whose turn it is, and the round.
- **Good to know:** your party has HP bars. Monsters show no numbers and no health word (ask the GM
  who is hurt; the person who runs the tool can switch on a rough one), and a creature whose name you
  have not learned is "Unknown creature". Hidden creatures are not listed at all.

## Read a handout

- **Do:** open **Handouts** on the player page and click a title. You can also find the same page
  in Foundry's Journal tab.
- **"just for you":** the GM revealed it to you (or a few of you) only. Other players do not see it
  on their page, and in Foundry only you can open it. Share it at the table if your character
  would.
- **Tip:** nothing new? Handouts appear within a few seconds of the GM revealing them. Reload the
  page if one seems missing.

## Check the quests and the party stash

- **Look at:** **Quests** on the player page. An open quest shows the day the GM revealed it, a
  finished one is struck through under **Done**, and a failed one has a **Failed** tag. The recap card
  starts with a one-line **Last time**.
- **Look at:** **Party stash**: what the party carries, with a search box and the group's gold. An item
  you have not identified shows only as "Unidentified item".
- **Good to know:** you see only what the GM has revealed. Nothing here is a spoiler.

## Write your downtime and your diary

Two optional fold-outs under your sheet on **My character**. Skip them if you like: the game works
the same.

- **Downtime:** write what your character does between sessions, for example "I visit the
  blacksmith and ask about the strange coin". You can keep up to ten short entries and edit or
  delete your own. Only you and your GM can read them, and the GM treats them as wishes, not as
  things that have happened.
- **My diary:** ask your Claude helper for a short diary entry in your character's voice about the
  last session (it asks you for one feeling first, and uses only what you have been shown), paste
  it into **Diary entry**, change it however you like and press **Save entry**. Only you can read
  your diary: not the GM, not the other players. If the page says the table keeps diaries in memory
  only, they disappear when the dashboard restarts, so copy anything you want to keep.
- **Good to know:** you need your private My character link for both. Nothing has to be switched
  on.

## Get an omen on Discord

Between sessions the GM may send each player a short private hint about what is coming. It arrives
as a private Discord message from the table's bot, only to you, and only after the GM has approved
it. You do not need to do anything. If you never get one, the GM may not have your Discord user id
yet, so tell them. Nobody else sees your message, and it is not on the player page.

## See what you can do on your turn

- **Look at:** the **On my turn** card on **My character**. It groups what your character can do into
  action, bonus action, reaction and free, so you do not have to remember. The switch **Show the "On my turn" card** at the bottom of the page
  hides it if you do not want it.

## Ask the Discord bot

- **Do:** in Discord type `/recap` for the last recap, `/npc <name>` for what the GM has told everyone
  about a person, or `/handouts` for the handouts revealed so far.
- **Good to know:** only you see the answer. It is the handout's own text, the same as the player page,
  and a name the GM has not revealed says "Nothing revealed yet".

## Know when the session is recorded

- **Good to know:** when the person who runs the tool types `/record start` in Discord, the voice
  chat is recorded until `/record stop`, and the bot posts a notice when it starts. If you said no,
  your track is deleted after each session and the notes are written without you.
- **Tip:** want something off the record? Ask the GM; see
  [having your data removed](README.md#having-your-data-removed).

## Ask the GM about the story and the table

For your own character and the rules, use the [D&D helper](dnd-helper.md) yourself. For anything
about the story or what the tool keeps track of at the table, ask the GM, who can look it up. Good
questions:

- "How much damage did I deal last session?" (the play stats per character)
- "Which handouts do we have so far?"
- "Can you send us a recap of last session?" (a spoiler-free recap made for players)
- "What did the letter from last week say again?"

## Roll when the GM asks

Roll in Foundry as usual: click the roll on your character sheet, or type it in the chat. That is
all you need.

As an optional shortcut, if you opened your private link, a roll the GM asks for also shows as a
button on the player page and on My character. It uses Foundry's own roll, so conditions and
advantage on your character apply, and the result is the same as rolling in Foundry. Ignore the
button if you prefer to roll in Foundry.

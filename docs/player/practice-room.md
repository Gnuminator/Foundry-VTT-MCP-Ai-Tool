# The practice room

Never used Foundry before? Before the real game, your GM can set up a **practice room**: a small
empty scene with a training dummy and a hero of your own. You try the buttons you will need at the
table, in your own time, with nobody watching the clock. Nothing you do there counts, and the
dummy cannot hurt anyone.

You do not need the GM in the room. When they have set it up, they tell you its name and open it.
Join the game as usual (see [Join the game](join.md)); once the GM has opened it, you land on the
practice scene.

## What you get

- A **practice hero** with your name on it. Of the players, only you can move it.
- A **training dummy** to attack. It has no attacks of its own.
- A short **step list** in the Journal tab (the book icon), called "Practice room: how to" (it has an English and a Danish page).
- A **combat tracker** that is already running, so you can see whose turn it is and how to end it.

## The four steps

1. **Move.** Drag your hero token a few squares towards the dummy. Or click it and use the arrow
   keys.
2. **Attack.** Open your hero's sheet (double-click the token), press the **Practice Sword** on the
   Inventory tab and choose **Attack**. Target the dummy first: click it, then press **T**. Then
   roll damage from the chat card.
3. **Cast.** On the Spells tab, press **Practice Spark**, a harmless cantrip, and roll its attack
   the same way.
4. **End your turn.** When the combat tracker says it is your turn, press the arrow at the bottom
   of the tracker (or the end-turn button on your token) to pass it on.

If several of you practise at once, the turns go round the heroes. Wait for yours, as you will in
the real game.

## When you are done

Tell the GM. They remove the room: the scene, the dummy, the practice heroes, the step list and
the combat all go away, and your real character is untouched. (Foundry's Undo works only before
anyone has played in the room; after that the GM deletes those few things by hand.)

The practice room is an extra. The normal way to learn Foundry is still playing it, and your GM
can always help.

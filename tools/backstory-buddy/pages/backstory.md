# Backstory flow

1. **Read the sheet.** `get-my-character` (ask which character if there are several). Note for
   yourself: species, class, background, alignment, any personality traits, ideals, bonds and
   flaws on the sheet, and notable features. These are your facts.
2. **Read what is revealed.** `get-my-handouts`. Note anything that touches this character (a
   place, a person, a recap line). If nothing is revealed, say the backstory will stand on its own
   and the GM can tie it in later.
3. **Ask two or three questions.** Examples: where did they grow up, who did they leave behind,
   what do they want that they have not got. Skip any the sheet already answers.
4. **Draft.** 150 to 250 words, in the third person unless the player prefers the first. Use the
   sheet's own traits and background as the spine. Mark invented details with "(idea)" the first
   time each appears.
5. **Check yourself.** Read the draft once as a stranger: does any name, place or power come from
   neither the sheet nor a revealed handout? If it does and the player did not supply it, remove
   it. Does a line contradict the sheet? Fix it.
6. **Hooks.** Add two or three open threads for the GM, each one sentence.
7. **Offer one round of edits,** then the note (`references/note-format.md`).

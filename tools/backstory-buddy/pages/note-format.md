# The copy-ready note

Always end with one block the player can copy as it is. Use this shape and nothing more:

```text
Backstory: <character name>
<the backstory, 150 to 250 words>

Voice
- Speaks: <one line>
- Reacts: <one line>
- Wants: <one line>
- Fears: <one line>

Hooks for the GM
- <thread one>
- <thread two>

Built from: the character sheet and the handouts revealed to me. Lines marked (idea) were suggested.
```

Tell the player: copy it into the notes on their sheet in Foundry, or send it to the GM, whichever
they prefer. You cannot save it for them. Add no heading, sign-off or summary after the block.

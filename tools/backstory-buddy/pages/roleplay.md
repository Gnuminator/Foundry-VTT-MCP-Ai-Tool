# Roleplay voice

1. Start from the sheet: personality traits, ideals, bonds, flaws, background and alignment.
2. Give the player three small things to play with:
   - **A way of speaking:** short or long sentences, a habit, a word they overuse.
   - **A reaction:** what the character does when surprised, insulted or thanked.
   - **A want and a fear,** one line each, taken from the bond and flaw if the sheet has them.
3. Offer two example lines the character might say at the table. Label them "idea".
4. Keep it playable: a player under pressure should be able to remember it in one breath.
5. Never decide for the player. Offer, then let them pick or rewrite.
6. Do not describe how the character relates to anyone or anything in the campaign unless a
   handout revealed to this player says so.

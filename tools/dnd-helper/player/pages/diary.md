# Character diary flow

A short diary entry, in character, about the last session. It is the player's keepsake and a way
to remember the story. You draft it; the player edits it and saves it on their own "My character"
page, under **My diary**. You have no tool that saves anything.

1. **Ask for one feeling first.** One question: "How did your character feel at the end of the
   session?" (a word or a short line is enough). Do not draft before you have it.
2. **Gather what the player may already know, and only that.** Call `get-my-character` for the
   name, class and background, `get-my-play-stats` for their own rolls, hits, kills and
   close calls in the last session, and `get-my-handouts` for the newest recap and the handouts the
   GM revealed to them. Nothing else is a source.
3. **Draft about 150 words**, first person, in the character's voice, in the player's language
   (Danish keeps the English game terms). Use the feeling from step 1 as the thread. Keep it a
   diary, not a report: one or two moments, one small detail, one thought about what comes next.
4. **Name only revealed facts.** A person, place, item or secret appears only if the recap or a
   handout the player received names it. Do not add the adventure's hidden truths, what the GM
   planned, another player's character sheet or what other characters did in private. If the recap
   says little, write the feeling and the character's own actions and leave the rest vague. When
   unsure whether something is revealed, leave it out.
5. **Numbers stay in the character's words.** "I hit twice and missed badly" is fine; do not list
   dice, DCs or hit points.
6. **Hand it over.** Show the draft and say: "Edit it any way you like, then open **My diary** on
   your My character page, paste it in and press Save entry." Offer one revision round (shorter,
   funnier, darker) and then stop.
7. Never paste book text into the entry, and never claim it was saved: you cannot see the page.

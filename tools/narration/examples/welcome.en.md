# Before the session

Welcome back. Before the players arrive, open Foundry and load the world.
On the left you will find the scene controls.

Select a token to see its hit points, and right-click it to change its disposition.
The dnd5e system rolls attacks and saving throws for you, so you only need to click.

# The Tarokka reading

[pause 0.5]
When the party reaches the fortune teller's camp, open the Tarokka drawer in the dashboard and draw the cards one by one.
If something goes wrong, every change the AI makes can be undone from the Recent Changes list.
